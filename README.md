# N‑body dynamics of neutron stars and black holes after supernova kicks

**Full report**: [View PDF](Nbody_SN_kicks_report.pdf)

## Summary
We simulated the dynamical evolution of 10⁴ high‑mass stars in a Milky‑Way‑like potential using `sevnpy` (stellar evolution) and `fireworks` (N‑body). We focused on post‑supernova kicks and compared the spatial distribution of neutron stars vs. black holes under different kick models (Hobbs, Hobbs‑pure) and metallicities (Z=0.02, Z=0.002).

## Key findings
- Neutron stars receive larger kicks → broader radial and vertical distribution.
- Black holes remain more concentrated near the galactic plane and centre.
- Lower metallicity and Hobbs kicks lead to wider dispersal.
